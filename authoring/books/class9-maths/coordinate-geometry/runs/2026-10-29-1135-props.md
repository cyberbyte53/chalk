# Coordinate Geometry: coordinatePlane built

The definition was approved (thread cmvt1adgqnip closed, with the person's choice in chat: Riya moves by steppers, not by dragging; the definition already had no drag, so nothing changed).

## Built

- Run bodies for every layer: the plane (name a part, the quadrant sweep, the number lines gliding until their zeroes meet, a figure's points one at a time), reading (snapped, kept on the plane, the quadrants visited), places (a tap makes a house Aman's; a number lights every house with it), walker (along, a turn, up, one line at a time, each line passed announced; she ends where the numbers say), clues (each narrows to a region, a line, one point, or nowhere), plotting (right or wrong, decimals to a quarter, the scale grows the window and announces what falls off), dice (seeded, walking line by line, a miss past the goal, landing sends the other home).
- One derived value added to clues: `still`, where the point can still be (the view shades it).
- The view: one frame (`FrameStage` + `planeFrame`), not Mafs: the player's own frame gives drags, keys and tags in the plane's units, and Mafs isn't in any active package. Houses, desks, crossings, stalls, Riya and the counters are small SVG pictures. Riya's numbers are steppers → and ↑ (min and max from the window, so no negatives in the town).
- Registered in `library/registry` (package.json, index.ts, modules.ts, styles.css); `pnpm catalogue` rewritten. There is no `library/README.md` in this checkout to list it in.

## The dice rule (thread cmvt4fa6103h)

The person chose A. A die that would take its coordinate past 10 isn't used, and the counter moves by the other alone; only when both would is the turn missed. Changed in `dice.ts` (the throw, its describe, the `missed` signal's words), with tests: from (10, 0) only green moves it; five seeded games from (10, 0) all reach (10, 10); both dice over is a miss. 44 tests; `pnpm check` passes. flow.md and props.md still say "an overshooting move is missed" in the book's words; the prop's own description now says what it does.
