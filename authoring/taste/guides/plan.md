# Guide: planning a chapter

Read by `plan-chapter` and `build-props` (for **Props**). Output: the planning files (`brief.md`, `source/outline.yaml`, `objectives.md`, `flow.md`; then `props.md`), one at a time, each approved by the person. Nothing is built until they are.

**Read the whole source first** — objectives, examples, activities, in-text questions, exercises, and any section a later syllabus removed.

**Lessons.** Split the chapter into as many lessons as it has ideas (Euclid's lemma and HCF by Euclid's algorithm are two). A direct application of an idea (the forms 3q, 3q+1, 3q+2) is pages inside that lesson, not a lesson; list it as a skill sub-tag so the learner model can track it. Keep the source's order unless there is a reason, and say it.

**Mission and characters.** One goal for the chapter, from the student's world (Light: a phone projector for movie night), pursued by recurring characters the student learns with; a subject's chapters share them and can continue each other's story. Lessons are stops on it. For each lesson, name its order: discover → state, or state → see it hold (see `pages.md`). Use the source's own examples as its events (a rear-view mirror example happens on the school bus). Failures move it forward (a flat mirror can't project). Sensitive topics (reproduction, reproductive health): no mission, neutral health-class frame, the source's tone.

**Props.** `look` at `library/props/` first (or read `library/catalogue.md`). Propose the fewest props that carry the chapter; for each, the mechanism it shows that text can't and the step the student does. Extending an existing prop beats a new one.

**Per lesson in the flow:** the idea's name as its heading, then its boards: each with the objectives it serves and the source it covers (figures, examples, exercises), what it is for, and what the student sees or does (in words, not prop names). New terms: 3–4 at most per lesson.

**Questions for the person.** The decisions only they can make, each a thread on the words it is about, with your recommendation. Few, and real.
