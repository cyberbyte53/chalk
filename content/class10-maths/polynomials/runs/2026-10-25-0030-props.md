# Props run, 2026-10-25: the chapter's open comments

**Extended: `valueMachine`** (review 20261024-1547, comments cmvlxqvbit3k, cmvlz6yqbcnd)
- New `move {x}` action: dragging the x-axis handle moves the working, with no dot and no signal. `put` is unchanged, so rules and witnesses still hold.
- The default step is 0.1 (it was 1), and up to 60 dots are kept (it was 24). The → button is gone.
- `machineNumber`: halves and quarters are written as fractions, other decimals as decimals (2.3, not 23/10).
- On a cubic, the value in the working goes on a second line.
- Law tests: decimal working, the 0.1 snap and range, and `move` leaving no dot.

**Built: `longDivision`** (comments cmvlzdps9wbl, cmvlzeswe4l2, cmvlzhqspy6z)
- Lays out the division as NCERT does, with like powers in columns. Each quotient term takes three moves: divide, multiply, subtract.
- The maths of the move just made is written under the layout.
- Law tests use NCERT Examples 6 and 7, plus Ex 2.3 Q2 (a remainder of 0), checking quotient placement, signals and addressability.

**Changed from the plan:** `polyBox` stays in the chapter for multiplying (zeroes-from-factors, zeroes-and-coefficients). Long division replaces it everywhere it divided: steps, when-to-stop, one-known, is-it-a-factor and the is-it-a-factor question family.
