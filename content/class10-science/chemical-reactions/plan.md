# Class 10 Science, Ch1: Chemical Reactions and Equations (plan)

Source: NCERT jesc101 (2020-21). Structure: theory-first, like Light L1. Each lesson is Hook (story) → Learn (NCERT order, define before use, props) → See it in life (story, explained) → Practice (examples, your turn, mission log). Hindi with every string.

## Mission: Diwali at home
Riya and Kabir get the house ready for Diwali. Each errand is a reaction from the chapter:
- whitewashing walls with lime (CaO + H2O, then CaCO3 shine)
- the rusty gate (corrosion, paint)
- Mom's silver anklets going black and the copper diyas going green
- keeping the namkeen fresh (rancidity, nitrogen)
- sparklers (Mg burning)

## Lessons
1. **What is a chemical reaction?** Physical vs chemical change; the four signs (change in state, colour, gas, temperature). Activities 1.1–1.3: the Mg ribbon, lead nitrate + KI, Zn + acid.
2. **Writing chemical equations.** Word equations; reactants and products; LHS/RHS; formulae; skeletal equations; conservation of mass as counting atoms.
3. **Balancing equations.** Hit-and-trial on Fe + H2O → Fe3O4 + H2. Change coefficients, never subscripts. State symbols (s)(l)(g)(aq); conditions on the arrow.
4. **Combination reactions + exothermic.** CaO + H2O (whitewash), Ca(OH)2 + CO2, coal, H2 + O2, CH4 burning, respiration, compost.
5. **Decomposition reactions + endothermic.**
   - by heat: FeSO4, CaCO3, Pb(NO3)2
   - by electricity: electrolysis of water, 2:1 volumes
   - by light: AgCl, AgBr in photography
   - endothermic: Ba(OH)2 + NH4Cl
6. **Displacement and double displacement.**
   - Fe + CuSO4, Zn and Pb vs Cu, reactivity
   - Na2SO4 + BaCl2: precipitate, exchange of ions; PbI2 recall
7. **Oxidation and reduction.** Cu → CuO → Cu; gain/loss of oxygen or hydrogen; redox. ZnO + C, MnO2 + HCl, Mg burning.
8. **Oxidation in everyday life.** Corrosion (rust, silver, copper) and rancidity (antioxidants, airtight, nitrogen-flushed chips). The mission finale.

## Props (new package @chalk/prop-chemistry)
- **equationBalancer**: coefficient steppers, an atom tally per element on both sides, molecule pictures; "balanced" derived.
- **reactionBench**: an animated test tube or beaker showing the observable signs: colour change, bubbles, precipitate, thermometer, flame or glow.
- **electrolysisCell**: two inverted tubes collecting H2 and O2 at 2:1.
- **particle animations**: displacement (Fe takes Cu's place), double displacement (ion swap), redox (oxygen moving from CuO to H2).
- **chemistryScene**: story illustrations.
