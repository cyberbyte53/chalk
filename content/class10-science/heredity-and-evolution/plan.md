# Class 10 Science, Ch9: Heredity and Evolution (plan)

Source: NCERT jesc109 (2020-21). Keep the whole chapter, including the evolution sections that the current syllabus cut. Structure: theory-first, like Light L1. Hindi with every string.

## Mission: Grandpa's terrace garden
Grandpa saved seeds from his tall pea plants, and some came up short. Riya and Kabir solve it the way Mendel did. NCERT's beetles-and-crows example then happens in the garden's bushes.

## Lessons
1. **Variation.** Variation accumulates over generations (Fig 9.1); asexual vs sexual diversity; not all variants survive equally; traits; the earlobe activity on the family.
2. **Mendel, one trait.** Pea characters; F1 all tall, so no blending; F2 3:1; factors = genes, two copies; dominant/recessive; TT/Tt/tt; the 1:2:1 ratio.
3. **Two traits.** Round/wrinkled × yellow/green; F1 all round yellow; F2 9:3:3:1 (315/108/101/32); independent inheritance.
4. **How traits are expressed.** DNA → protein, a gene for each protein; enzyme → hormone → height. Two gene sets; germ cells carry one set; chromosomes are separate pieces, which is why traits are inherited independently.
5. **Sex determination.** Environmental cues (reptiles' temperature, snails changing sex); 22 pairs + XX/XY; the father's chromosome decides; half boys, half girls.
6. **Evolution.** The beetles:
   - green beetles and crows: natural selection, adaptation
   - blue beetles and the elephant: genetic drift in small populations
   - starving beetles and cut mouse tails: acquired traits are not inherited
   - Darwin and Mendel; the origin of life (Haldane, Miller–Urey)
7. **Speciation.** Sub-populations, gene flow, a river isolating them, drift and selection acting differently, reproductive isolation.
8. **Evolution and classification.** Hierarchy of characteristics; common ancestry (siblings vs cousins); homologous vs analogous organs; fossils, how they form in layers, and how they're dated.
9. **Evolution by stages, and not "progress".** Eye-spots (Planaria), feathers, wild cabbage and artificial selection, molecular phylogeny, a tree not a ladder, human evolution out of Africa, no biological races.

## Props (new package @chalk/prop-heredity)
- **crossSimulator**: pick the parents' genotypes for one or two traits, see the Punnett square and the gametes, grow N plants and count the phenotype ratios.
- **chromosomeShuffle**: chromosome pairs separating into germ cells, then fusing at fertilisation; XY mode for sex determination.
- **beetlePopulation**: a population over generations with crows, an elephant event and food scarcity; a river split for speciation.
- **limbBones**: human arm, bat wing, bird wing, whale flipper; colour-matched bones (homologous vs analogous).
- **fossilLayers**: dig down through rock layers, older deeper.
- **heredityScene**: story illustrations.
