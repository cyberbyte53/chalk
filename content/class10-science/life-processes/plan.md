## Mission: Kabir's 1500 m at Sports Day
Kabir has signed up for the 1500 m race at school Sports Day, three weeks away. On his first practice run he is out of breath in two rounds, his legs cramp, and he comes home starving and thirsty. Riya (Class 10) becomes his coach: "To train your body, first find out what it does all day — even when you're asleep." Each lesson is one job the body does to stay alive and to run: fuel in (nutrition), burning the fuel (respiration), delivery (transport), clean-up (excretion). Grandpa's terrace garden (from the heredity chapter) shows the plant version of each job: the plants do all the same work without moving. Finale: race day — Kabir finishes, and explains every part of it to Grandpa.

Roadmap stops: (1) what "alive" needs, (2) plants make food, (3) how others get food, (4) our digestion, (5) burning food for energy, (6) breathing, (7) heart and blood, (8) plants' pipes, (9) clean-up: kidneys — race day.

## Lessons
1. **What are life processes?** Alive vs not (movement, molecular movement, viruses); order breaks down → maintenance; the four life processes; why diffusion is not enough for a big body → need for organs and transport. Prop: diffusionReach. Hook: the sign-up sheet; "even asleep your body is working". In life: why Kabir needs a heart and lungs but an amoeba doesn't.
2. **Photosynthesis (autotrophic nutrition).** Autotroph/heterotroph; enzymes; the equation; three events; chloroplasts; starch store (and glycogen in us); Activity 6.1 variegated leaf; stomata and guard cells; Activity 6.2 KOH bell-jars; raw materials from soil (water, N as nitrates). Prop: leafLab (variegated steps, stomata, bell-jars). In life: Grandpa's money plant and why his garden needs sun.
3. **How other organisms get food (heterotrophic).** Food outside vs inside the body (fungi/yeast/mushroom vs whole food), parasites (cuscuta/amar-bel, ticks, lice, leeches, tapeworms), Amoeba (pseudopodia, food vacuole), Paramoecium (cilia). Prop: cellFeeding. In life: the amar-bel on Grandpa's hedge; bread mould.
4. **Digestion in human beings.** Alimentary canal; teeth, saliva, salivary amylase (Activity 6.3); peristalsis; oesophagus; stomach (HCl, pepsin, mucus, acidity); sphincter; small intestine (longest; herbivore vs carnivore), bile (alkaline, emulsifies fat), pancreatic juice (trypsin, lipase), intestinal juice → glucose, amino acids, fatty acids + glycerol; villi; large intestine; anus; dental caries. Prop: digestiveTract (journey + saliva test). In life: Kabir's pre-race breakfast — what happens to each part of it.
5. **Respiration.** Activity 6.4 (lime water: breath vs pichkari), 6.5 (yeast); glucose → pyruvate (cytoplasm); yeast → ethanol + CO₂ (anaerobic, fermentation); mitochondria → CO₂ + water (aerobic, far more energy); muscle → lactic acid (cramps); ATP as energy currency; plants' gas exchange by day and night. Prop: respirationPaths (pathways + lime-water test). In life: Kabir's cramp explained.
6. **Breathing.** Aquatic vs terrestrial (dissolved oxygen, faster breathing; Activity 6.6 fish), large thin protected surfaces; nostrils, hairs, mucus, throat rings, bronchi, alveoli; breathing in (ribs lift, diaphragm flattens); exchange at the alveoli; residual volume; haemoglobin; 80 m²; tobacco and smoking. Prop: breathing (chest, alveolus, fish). In life: why Kabir pants after a sprint.
7. **Heart and blood.** Blood (plasma, red blood corpuscles), the heart's four chambers step by step, valves, thick ventricles, double circulation (vs fish two-chambered, amphibians three-chambered), blood pressure (120/80), arteries/veins/capillaries, platelets, lymph. Prop: heart (steps, circulation, fish, vessels). In life: Kabir's pulse before and after running.
8. **Transport in plants.** Why plants need it; xylem and phloem; root pressure; transpiration and transpiration pull (Activity 6.8), day vs night; translocation in phloem (sieve tubes, companion cells, energy from ATP, both directions, spring buds). Prop: plantTransport (water, food, bag). In life: Grandpa's wilting peas at noon.
9. **Excretion.** Human excretory system; nephron (glomerulus, Bowman's capsule, tubule); selective reabsorption; regulation of urine; 180 L filtrate vs 1–2 L urine; artificial kidney (dialysis); organ donation; excretion in plants. Prop: nephron (system, filter, water, dialysis). Finale: race day.

- **respirationPaths** — glucose → pyruvate → aerobic / yeast / muscle, with where it happens and an energy meter; lime-water test (breath, pichkari, yeast).
- **breathing** — chest breathing in/out (ribs, diaphragm, lung volume, air path labels), an alveolus gas-exchange close-up, a fish's gills.

- **plantTransport** — water up the xylem (day: transpiration pull; night: root pressure), food in the phloem (summer vs spring), plant vs stick under plastic.
- **nephron** — the excretory system, a nephron step by step (filter, reabsorb, urine), water intake vs urine volume, dialysis.
