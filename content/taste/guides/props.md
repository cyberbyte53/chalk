# Guide: building props

Read by `build-props`. The contract and code rules are in `platform/docs/making-a-prop.md`; this is the teaching side.

- **Earn the place.** A prop shows a mechanism text can't, and the student does the step. If it only illustrates, draw a picture instead. Check `library/catalogue.md`; extend before you add.
- **One visual per idea.** Fewer modes and buttons. Every control labelled in words; every drag handle explained once.
- **Big enough to see.** What the text talks about is visible and labelled at phone size (mirror scale marked, not just 0 and 100 cm).
- **Things that differ look different.** Angle of incidence and reflection in distinct colours; image and object recognisably the same thing.
- **Fixed scale for change.** A prop teaching change keeps its scale while the value moves.
- **Their own numbers** after the guided case; inputs fine-grained enough to see the pattern form (more x points, then the curve).
- **Show what matters about the result** (an image's height, not just its position).
- **Hide undefined marks** until the lesson defines them (props take that as a part/option).
- **Jugnu-ready:** state and observation describe what the student sees; every change is an action. Content never depends on Jugnu.
