# Prop catalogue

Generated by `pnpm catalogue` from the prop definitions (each prop's contract: its code) and the content tree; do not edit. Each prop: its address, its role, what it is for and its world (its `describe`), its fields, its actions and its signals, and the chapters that place it. A field no action changes is a setting, fixed where a board places the prop; an input is fed by a board's rules. `look` at a prop's address for the same page with a picture and every board that uses it, and `look` at `library/props/` for one line per prop.

Before writing a new prop, look here: a chapter is better served by a prop that exists, reused, than by a new one-off.

A plane located by two perpendicular lines, drawn only as far as the lesson has defined it: a town's main road and a street counted from 1, a sheet's edges, or the x- and y-axes as number lines crossing at O, with their directions and quadrants. ## `decimal` — `@chalk/prop-decimal`

Turning a fraction p/q into a decimal by long division, one digit at a time. Each step multiplies the remainder by 10 and divides by q; the remainder is always less than q, so either a remainder becomes 0 (the expansion terminates) or a remainder comes back and the digits repeat from there (non-terminating recurring). It knows division, nothing about any lesson.

### `tenMaker` · explainer
